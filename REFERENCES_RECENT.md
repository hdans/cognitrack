1. Serap Kırbız (2025). "Improving Facial Emotion Recognition through Dataset Merging and Balanced Training Strategies." Journal of the Franklin Institute. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfranklin.2025.107659
   - Relevansi: Strategi training dan penggabungan dataset untuk meningkatkan generalisasi dan stabilitas model FER.

2. Laura Rayón Ropero, Jasper De Laet, Filip Lemic, et al. (2026). "Towards Emotion Recognition with 3D Pointclouds Obtained from Facial Expression Images." IEEE Transactions on Affective Computing. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/TAFFC.2026.3679039
   - Relevansi: Representasi 3D/geomterik untuk robustness terhadap pose dan occlusion — relevan jika pose mengganggu prediksi emosi.

3. ANK Zaman, Prosenjit Chatterjee, Rajat Sharma (2024). "Real-Time Drivers' Drowsiness Detection and Analysis through Deep Learning." Proc. 2nd Int. Conf. on AI, Blockchain & IoT (AIBThings). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/AIBThings63359.2024.10861885
   - Relevansi: Kombinasi EAR + head‑pose untuk deteksi drowsiness/attention; metodologi applicable ke engagement tracking.

4. William Lindskog, Valentin Spannagl, Christian Prehofer (2024). "Federated Learning for Drowsiness Detection in Connected Vehicles." Lecture Notes / Conference Proc. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-49379-9_9
   - Relevansi: Pendekatan temporal eye metrics & privasi; berguna untuk deployment skala besar dan privasi siswa.

5. (Preprint) "Improving Driver Drowsiness Detection via Personalized EAR/MAR Thresholds and CNN‑Based Classification" (2026). arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.22479
   - Relevansi: Pendekatan personalisasi threshold EAR, mendukung argumentasi kalibrasi per‑user.

6. (Preprint) "Blinking Beyond EAR: A Stable Eyelid Angle Metric for Driver Drowsiness Detection and Data Augmentation" (2025). arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.19519
   - Relevansi: Metrik alternatif yang mengurangi noise pada deteksi mata; ide untuk stabilisasi fitur EAR.

7. (Preprint) "Deep Learning‑Based Real‑Time Sequential Facial Expression Analysis Using Geometric Features" (2025). arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.05669
   - Relevansi: Analisis sekuensial/temporal untuk konsistensi label dan mengurangi jitter di FER.

8. (Preprint) "Emotion Diffusion Classifier with Adaptive Margin Discrepancy Training for Facial Expression Recognition" (2026). arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.29578
   - Relevansi: Metode classifier robust yang dapat meningkatkan stabilitas prediksi multi‑kelas.

9. (Preprint) "Automatic Mind Wandering Detection in Educational Settings: A Systematic Review and Multimodal Benchmarking" (2026). arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09569
   - Relevansi: Review modern terkait mind‑wandering/attention detection di pendidikan — berguna sebagai landasan metodologi.

10. (Conference) "InsideOut: An EfficientNetV2-S Based Deep Learning Framework for Robust Multi-Class Facial Emotion Recognition" (2025). arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.03066
   - Relevansi: Arsitektur efisien dan teknik robustness untuk FER yang dapat dipertimbangkan jika perlu model ringan.
